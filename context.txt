There is one limitation: we should not try to programmatically force-add the customer to a WhatsApp group. The safer setup is to automatically send them the WhatsApp group invite link after payment. They click it and join themselves.

This is actually better operationally because you don't have to manually add every participant.

I recommend Razorpay

For this workshop, use Razorpay Payment Links rather than a generic payment URL.

Razorpay supports creating Payment Links through its API, and each Payment Link can be tracked against the customer/payment.

Also, Razorpay says a Payment Link is intended for a single customer, so our Apps Script should generate a new ₹1,499 payment link for every registration rather than putting one common payment link in the form.
